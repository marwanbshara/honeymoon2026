Pro Tips

From the guide who's helped hundreds of couples — the things that actually make the difference.

Cancún
Concierge = first stopWalk to concierge before unpacking. Book ALL excursions in the first hour of arrival.
No sunscreen before dolphinsDelphinus will ask you to remove sunscreen. Wear a rash guard instead.
Tulum: leave by 7:30 amRuins get crowded by 10 am. Private driver, leave resort 7:30 am sharp.
Nap before Coco BongoDay 4 is activity + late night. A 2-hr nap at 2 pm makes everything better.
Mention honeymoon everywhereTell every vendor, restaurant and hotel. Upgrades, roses and free desserts happen constantly.
Cash floatBring $120–150 USD cash for tips, cenote lockers and market shopping.
Las Vegas
Book cabanas earlyJuly heat is 105°F+. A cabana with shade at EBC or TAO Beach is worth every dollar.
Join free guest listsXS Night Swim, EBC and most dayclubs have free online guest lists — always join before going.
4th of July is wildJul 4 is the biggest night in Vegas. Book XS guest list and TAO Beach cabana days ahead.
Hit buffets at openingBacchanal at 8 am to avoid the 45-min line. Food is freshest at opening too.
Fremont is freeThe canopy light show runs every hour 6 pm–2 am — completely free and spectacular.
Hawaii
Hanauma Bay = set an alarmReserve at exactly 7 am HST on Sunday Jul 5 for Tue Jul 7. Sells out in under 5 minutes.
Fireworks are on your beachFri Jul 10 — best seat in the house is your own Hilton beach. Cocktail + sand.
La Mer dress codeSmart attire required — collared shirt + trousers for men. No shorts. Book on arrival.
Wild dolphins vs CancúnOahu dolphins approach you freely in the wild. Profoundly different and more magical.
Ask Hilton about luau discountHotel guests sometimes get priority seating and a small discount on the Starlight Luau.
Reef-safe sunscreen onlyRequired at Hanauma Bay — they will turn you away at the gate with regular sunscreen.
